Transparent Pricing

Rarely do speakers share pricing transparently, for fear it will lose them potential opportunities before they ever get the chance to talk to them. But as someone who speaks about and studies communication, the data is pretty clear: being upfront is better than playing games. So I’d rather err on the side of transparency, and at least provide an idea of what it costs to work with me. If you want to work with me, even if there is a budgetary constraint, reach out! I’m happy to talk to you about your event and sometimes we can make it work.

You’ll immediately notice, I don’t charge every event the exact same amount. Some events require immense custom planning and research, and take multiple days off the calendar. Others are simple and close to home. I try to work with my clients to meet them where their budget is at, which means every event looks a little different.

$10K-$20K

Premium clients. This price range represents deeply customized content, events that encompass multiple programs across multiple days, major conferences, and international engagements.

$7K-$10K

Standard Rate. This is what I would quote a client if I had no additional context about their event and what they needed. Most clients looking for conference keynotes, staff trainings, or corporat/leadership gatherings should expect a quote in this range. 

$4K-$7K

Exceptions. Discounted pricing is selectively available to organizations or causes I believe in and want to support, as well as events with non-monetary factors that justify the discount.

< $4K

Rare Exceptions. Every now and then, I will work with organizations that have incredibly limited budget (or no budget at all!). These are generally non-profits or have other extenuating circumstances in play. But if your event falls in this description, it never hurts to ask!

I’ve long hated the negotiation song-and-dance, and like to operate under the philosophy that if you shoot straight with me, I’ll shoot straight with you. I want to be a partner in your event, and that’s much easier when we can communicate effectively with each other.

If you appreciate that, then we’ll probably work really well together!

If you have question, concerns, or just want to get started, reach out with the form below, and we can talk it through. I guarantee you’ll get a response within 24 hours, and often you’ll get one so fast that it will be downright unsettling.
